About

I'm Maciej. A father of three, I love travelling, and for 15+ years I've been building software and training teams.

Our children learn at home. So every day I practise what I teach teams: explaining so that someone truly understands, not just hears.

I work on myself a lot. I practise Nonviolent Communication (NVC), because good decisions in a team start with a conversation in which nobody has to defend themselves.

Who I am at work

Five CliftonStrengths talents, and what you get from them.

  • LearnerI get into a domain and a system I don't know quickly.
  • IndividualizationI fit the way of working to the people in the team, not to a template.
  • StrategicI see several paths and choose the one that leads to the goal.
  • AnalyticalI ask for evidence and data before I change anything.
  • MaximizerI'd rather make what works excellent than start over.

How I work

I take on what everyone agrees matters and nobody starts. Before I begin, I look at what surrounds the task, and sometimes I widen the scope to do it better. When a financial report needed a more precise method, I built a separate endpoint instead of patching the old one and agreed it with another team. The precision of the data went up significantly, and the company now makes decisions on better information.

  1. ReconI map the domain and the pain, so we fix the right thing.
  2. ActionI do what is needed together with your team, in the code, not from an ivory tower.
  3. HandoverDocumentation and training, so the team copes without me.

Proof

Most of the value I add is not code I write myself, but the level I raise the team to through code review and mentoring. Several engineers I have led now lead their own teams.
