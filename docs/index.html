Questions and answers

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

An engineer who doesn't stop at recommendations. I do the recon, go where it hurts most, do what needs doing, and for the length of the mission I can work inside your company. I leave when the team runs on its own.

How is a fractional CTO different from a full-time CTO?

You get a technical leader for part of the time, without the cost of a full-time CTO. I learn your goals, help set the technical direction and the KPIs, then lead the team, getting into the code when needed.

Is the training on-site or online?

Either: at your place or online. I write the programme to fit your team after a short conversation about your expectations.

How does working together look?

Three steps: recon (a map of the domain and the pain), action (working together with your team) and handover (documentation and training, so the team works without me).
